# Reraise

**The model is replaceable. Your world is not.**

Reraise is a layer of your own between you and any AI model. What you know lies on cards, each with its source. You and your agents read a situation on one board. And people trade cards with each other.

Reraise in ninety seconds: the film on [reraise.ai](https://reraise.ai/). The whole idea in twenty short chapters: [reraise.ai/film/](https://reraise.ai/film/).

## The card

One statement, with where it comes from. A card keeps three things apart: what is proven, what was only said, and what you believe. What you believe is yours alone and never leaves your device. [How a card is built](https://reraise.ai/cards/)

## The board

Cards alone are a pile. On the board they become a situation: who the players are, what drives them, what was known when. Your agents lay cards; only you mark what you believe. [Open the board](https://reraise.ai/board/) (example data) · [Board and fields](https://reraise.ai/board-and-fields/)

## The trade

People bring in what is publicly findable, with its source, and take what they need. The origin stays on every card, so ten thousand copies of one wire story count as one source. Your conclusions and your beliefs never travel. [Open the exchange](https://reraise.ai/trade/) · [Trading rules](https://reraise.ai/trading-rules/)

## Why

The big houses made their bet: that you would hand them your world. This is the reraise. The sixth estate is being decided now: a handful of models that answer for everyone, or many independent people who keep their own cards and compare them. [Read the whole argument](https://reraise.ai/why/)

## Join in

Get your number, bring in your first card, bring your field, or build with us here. The tool and the card format are open source and free to use. [Everyone can play](https://reraise.ai/#join)

## What exists today

- The card format v2: specification and file format, in [Reraise-ai/reraise](https://github.com/Reraise-ai/reraise).
- The tool (board app, API, MCP server): works on a developer machine with an example world; not released yet.
- A clickable board demo with example data: a gold market, a vitamin D claim, a Hormuz situation map.
- A first exchange in beta, where every trading partner gets a running number.
- The films: ninety seconds, and thirteen minutes in twenty chapters.

## Not built yet

Private trades from device to device · a track record for sources · role play with agents as players · card tiers · payments · connectors for individual systems as finished products.
